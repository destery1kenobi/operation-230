Sync across devices

Your progress is saved to a secret GitHub Gist, so your phone, tablet, and laptop all see the same day.

One-time setup
  1. Go to github.com/settings/tokens/new — the link pre-checks the right box.
  2. Confirm only gist is checked. Nothing else.
  3. Set an expiration you'll remember — 90 days or 1 year.
  4. Generate it, copy the token, paste it below, and press Connect.
